Goodnight

Play on Itch.io

Game Overview

Goodnight is an immersive 2D narrative exploration game developed in Unity. Dive into a surreal world of dreams, unraveling a deeply personal story through atmospheric exploration and subtle puzzle-solving.

Game Objectives

  • Create an introspective journey through interconnected dreamscapes
  • Provide a unique, atmospheric narrative experience
  • Explore themes of memory, perception, and subconsciousness
  • Encourage player interpretation and emotional engagement

Technical Details

Developed using Unity and C#, leveraging advanced storytelling techniques and minimalist game design principles to create an immersive experience.

Artistic Inspiration

Drawing inspiration from cult classics like Yume Nikki and Undertale, Goodnight explores narrative depth through environmental storytelling and symbolic game design.
